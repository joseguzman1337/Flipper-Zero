<summary>📦 <b>Community Repositories, Databases & Payloads</b></summary>

- 🌟 **[awesome-flipperzero](https://github.com/djsime1/awesome-flipperzero)** — Curated directory of Flipper Zero apps, games, tools, and docs.

- 📡 **[Flipper-IRDB](https://github.com/logickworkshop/Flipper-IRDB)** — Comprehensive crowd-sourced infrared database.
- 🔌 **[DrB0rk Expansion Boards](https://github.com/DrB0rk/Flipper-Zero-Boards)** — Custom add-on boards, schematics, and pinout diagrams.
- 🎵 **[FlipperMusicRTTTL](https://github.com/neverfa11ing/FlipperMusicRTTTL)** & **[flipper-music-files](https://github.com/Tonsil/flipper-music-files)** — RTTTL ringtones and audio playback files.
- 👾 **[FlipperAmiibo](https://github.com/Gioman101/FlipperAmiibo)** — Amiibo NFC dumps and database.
- 🎨 **[Flipper Maker](https://flippermaker.github.io/)** — Online creator tool for animations and icons.
- ⌨️ **[Flipper BadUSB Payloads (I-Am-Jakoby)](https://github.com/I-Am-Jakoby/Flipper-Zero-BadUSB)** — Advanced DuckyScript payloads.
- ⌨️ **[Generic BadUSB Payloads](https://github.com/nocomp/Flipper_Zero_Badusb_hack5_payloads)** — Hak5 style multi-platform payloads.
- ⌨️ **[FalsePhilosopher BadUSB](https://github.com/FalsePhilosopher/badusb)** — Additional BadUSB scripts.
- 📺 **[FlipperZero-TouchTunes](https://github.com/jimilinuxguy/flipperzero-touchtunes)** — TouchTunes jukebox remote signals.
- 🔑 **[T119 Bruteforcer](https://github.com/xb8/t119bruteforcer)** & **[flipperzero-bruteforce](https://github.com/tobiabocchi/flipperzero-bruteforce)** — Sub-GHz brute-forcing utilities.
- ✨ **[Flipper-StarNew](https://github.com/GlUTEN-BASH/Flipper-Starnew)** & **[FlipperZero-Goodies](https://github.com/wetox-team/flipperzero-goodies)** — Community tweaks, scripts, and media.
- 🔄 **[SerialHex2FlipperZeroInfrared](https://github.com/maehw/SerialHex2FlipperZeroInfrared)** — Hex converter for infrared files.
